# PodifyAI 15s Brand Film Design

## Style Prompt

Create a clean, premium, young tech brand short that matches the local PodifyAI website. Use a light canvas, black interface language, a restrained pink brand accent, subtle purple waveform cues, rounded white UI surfaces, thin data lines, and quiet motion. The film should feel like an AI podcast studio, not a loud SaaS ad.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#fbfbfd`
- Text: `#050505`
- Muted text: `#5f6068`
- Border: `#e7e7ee`
- Brand pink: `#e91e63`
- Accessible pink text: `#ad174d`
- Wave purple: `#6f55d9`
- Soft panel: `#ffffff`

## Typography

- Primary: `"Inter", "PingFang SC", "Microsoft YaHei", Arial, sans-serif`
- The product website uses Inter, so the film keeps it for brand consistency.
- Use large, sparse text. No paragraph blocks.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not add a dark cyberpunk palette.
- Do not use dense text or feature lists.
- Do not invent marketing copy beyond the local website language.
- Do not use noisy gradients, neon clutter, or generic stock visuals.
- Do not cover screenshots with heavy effects.
