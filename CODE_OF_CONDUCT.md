# Code of Conduct

PodifyAI welcomes constructive participation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections when impact and intent differ.
- Keep issue and pull request discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask to stop.
- Sharing exploit details publicly instead of following the security policy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

Security-sensitive issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
